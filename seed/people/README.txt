Drop your AI person images here (jpg/png/webp). Name them 01..10 (any names work,
they're read in sorted order). The seed script assigns them like this:

  - first 5 images  -> curator profile photos (Lena, Noah, Sofia, Amara, Mira)
  - remaining images -> try-on posts (a person "wearing" a look, attributed to a
                        curator, shown in the feed/Discover)

Tip: portraits / half-body work best for the 5 curator photos; full-body fashion
shots work best for the try-on posts. If you have a mix, name the portraits first
(01-05) and the full-body ones after (06-10).
